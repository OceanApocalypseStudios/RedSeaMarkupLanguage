# Proposal Contribution Guideline
Proposal contribution guideline for **Red Sea Modern Language** (or RSML, for short).

## Who is this for?
This is for anyone who wants to contribute with RSML feature proposals.

## Rules
* **You're writing a proposal, not a request.** I never liked the term request, because it implies "I want this done and I want it now". What you're writing is a proposal: it will be analyzed and, ultimately, there will be a decision on whether to accept it or not.
* **Explain thoroughly.** Do not write "add X feature". Instead, explain why that feature would be helpful and/or what it's trying to improve/fix. Then, add sections explaining possible syntax, how the analyzer would look at it and, if you want to be extra kind, some code style rules that should be followed when using X feature. Lastly, add a section discussing potential drawbacks or losses associated with said feature.
* **Don't walk away.** Don't drop the document and walk away! When you contribute with the document, a GitHub discussion is then initiated to discuss it. Partake in it! Clear doubts! After all, it's your contribution.
* **Listen.** This is perhaps the most important one. You need to open to criticism. Chances are high we won't agree with everything you write in your proposal. Do not take it as a personal attack. Chances are *also* high we'll agree with several points! Even if your proposal ends up being rejected, do not blame yourself and do not blame our team.
* **Write in English, please.** We also understand other languages, such as Portuguese, but pleas ekeep all conversation in English for simplicity. This includes proposals.
* **Language-only proposals.** Do not propose API features -- only language features.
* **Right to reject.** It's our right to reject your proposal. We always try to justify on why we reject, but please keep in mind we do not have to justify ourselves on this matter.
* **Do not double-propose or propose implemented/rejected/inactive content.**
* **Be kind and respect everyone.**

## How to contribute?
Before proceeding, make sure your proposal is different enough from all rejected and inactive proposals. In addition, make sure your proposal hasn't been already implemented.

Lastly, if you want to propose multiple things and they're aren't related to each other, it's recommended to split the proposals across files.

In the `templates` directory (inside `lang-design`), you'll find several templates. Pick the one that fits your goal better. You might also start a proposal from scratch, but we recommend you don't.

### After the proposal is written
1. Fork this repository (refer to the `dev` branch if the proposal is considered urgent; otherwise, refer to `main`).
2. Add your proposal to `lang-design/proposals/current`.
3. Create a Pull Request with a decent title -- something with keywords `Proposal` and `Language`.

And that's it! Just like that, you've contributed to the Red Sea Project.
